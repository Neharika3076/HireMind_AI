Questions

Answers, before you ask twice.

A clearer hiring map means fewer unknowns. Explore the answers teams ask before they take their first role global.

How fast can we get started?

Most teams import their first pipeline and post a role within a day. Full regional compliance setup typically takes a week.

Which countries does TerraHire support?

Compliant contracts and pay-band guidance are available in 27 countries today, with new regions added quarterly.

Can we migrate our existing pipelines?

Yes — bulk import from spreadsheets or common ATS exports, with a guided mapping step and no downtime.

Does TerraHire integrate with our tools?

TerraHire connects to common calendar, payroll and communication tools, and exposes an open API for anything else.

What does pricing look like?

Pricing scales with active roles rather than seats, so growing interview panels never inflate your bill.
