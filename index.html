Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know.

How do employees log into their portal?

Employees can log in using either their work email or their assigned project code (e.g. BILL-01).

Can an Admin create a new organization and project on the fly?

Yes! The built-in multi-step wizard allows creating a new organization or selecting an existing one, then mapping projects and managers instantly.

Are employee profiles editable?

Yes, employees can update their contact phone, location, and bio from their personal portal at any time.
